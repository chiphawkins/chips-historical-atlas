Data quality

Historical snapshots are approximate reconstructions and may omit duchies, principalities or city-states. A snapshot does not imply comprehensive coverage. Modern borders are a contemporary reference, not historical claims.

Modern polygons: Natural Earth via geo-countries (public domain). Historical polygons: Historical Basemaps (GPL-3.0). Basemap tiles: OpenStreetMap contributors / CARTO or OpenTopoMap. This demonstration loads remote data and requires an internet connection.
